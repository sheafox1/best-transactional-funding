How Transactional Funding Works

Your purchase and sale stand on their own

For wholesale double closings that can’t be assigned or joint ventured.

A to B

You get the property under contract with your seller, the A in the deal.

B to C

You find your end-buyer, the C, who wires their purchase funds to the closing agent.

We fund the A to B leg

Our funds close your purchase, usually for less than a day, and you close the sale the same day.
